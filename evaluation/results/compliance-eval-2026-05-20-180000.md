### Consent & Lawful Basis

#### consent-01 WhatsApp Opt-In Consent — Score: 7/10 ▲+3 (was 4/10)

**Evidence**:
- `src/pages/Home/NewsletterModal.jsx:9` — `const [consented, setConsented] = useState(false)`
- `src/pages/Home/NewsletterModal.jsx:88` — `setConsented(false)` on successful submission (resets state)
- `src/pages/Home/NewsletterModal.jsx:170–186`:
  ```jsx
  <div className="newsletter-modal__consent">
    <label className="newsletter-modal__consent-label">
      <input
        type="checkbox"
        checked={consented}
        onChange={(e) => setConsented(e.target.checked)}
        disabled={submitting}
        required
      />
      <span>
        I agree to receive WhatsApp messages about services and events from FGC Upper Room.{' '}
        <a href="/privacy" className="newsletter-modal__privacy-link">
          Privacy Policy
        </a>
      </span>
    </label>
  </div>
  <Button type="submit" variant="primary" size="lg" disabled={submitting || !consented}>
  ```
- `src/pages/Home/NewsletterModal.jsx:187` — submit button disabled while `!consented`
- `/privacy` route live at `src/App.jsx:141`

**Findings**: The P0.1 finding from the 120000 report (no consent checkbox at point of data collection) is now fully resolved. Users cannot submit the newsletter form without actively checking the consent box. The consent text specifically names WhatsApp messaging ("I agree to receive WhatsApp messages") and links directly to the Privacy Policy. The checkbox has `required` (HTML5 form validation) and the submit button is additionally `disabled` until `consented === true` (React-level enforcement). The Privacy Policy link opens the correct `/privacy` route.

Score rises 4 → 7. Not 8 or higher because:
- The `consented_at` timestamp is still set server-side as `now()` when the subscription is processed, not explicitly passed as a client timestamp from when the checkbox was checked. Under NDPR, best practice is to record the exact moment the user gave consent — this is a minor gap.
- The `subscribeApi` call does not forward a `consentedAt` field to the server, so the server cannot store a precise consent timestamp distinct from the registration timestamp.

**Remaining remediation**:
1. Pass `consentedAt: new Date().toISOString()` in the `subscribeVisitor(formData)` call when the form is submitted.
2. Server-side: store the received `consentedAt` rather than defaulting to `now()` (or document that `created_at` is used as a proxy for `consented_at`).

### NDPR-Specific Requirements

#### ndpr-01 NDPR Compliance Basics — Score: 8/10 ▲+1 (was 7/10)

**Evidence**:
- `src/pages/Home/NewsletterModal.jsx:170–186` — consent mechanism now explicit at point of collection
- NDPR Article 2.1(a): "personal data shall be collected only for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes" — consent checkbox now names the purpose ("WhatsApp messages about services and events")
- NDPR Article 2.1(b): "data must be adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary" — checkbox specifically scoped to WhatsApp messaging (not email, not third-party marketing)

**Findings**: The WhatsApp opt-in consent gap was the most significant NDPR weakness at 120000. With an explicit consent checkbox at point of collection, all six NDPR principles now have meaningful coverage:
1. Lawfulness — consent explicitly obtained at point of collection
2. Fairness — purpose stated in checkbox text
3. Transparency — Privacy Policy linked at point of collection
4. Purpose limitation — checkbox text scopes consent to WhatsApp messaging only
5. Data minimization — name, phone, email collected for stated purpose
6. Security — SSL, encrypted backups, incident response plan in place

Score rises 7 → 8. Held below 9 because the purge is not scheduled and the consent timestamp is not explicitly captured client-side.
